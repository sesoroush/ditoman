What you should knowآنچه باید بدانی

  • TMN has no meaningful liquidity or trading activity right now.
  • Supply is highly concentrated. Most of it sits in founder-controlled wallets, visible to anyone on BscScan.
  • The planned utility is not built yet.
  • The project is run by its two founders, at small scale.
  • TMN is not listed on CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap.
  • Several unrelated tokens use the ticker TMN. Always check the full contract address.

We state these plainly because our token-check lesson teaches people to look for exactly this.

Historyتاریخچه‌ی توکن

TMN is not a new launch. Its age and its quiet years are recorded on-chain.

  1. February 2022

    TMN is deployed

    The DiToman token is created on BNB Smart Chain, named after the Toman. You can check the creation date yourself on the BscScan contract page.

  2. 2022 to 2025

    The quiet years

    No marketing, no promotion, no token sale. These years were spent working out what Ditoman should actually be, through several drafts of the whitepaper.

  3. 2026

    The foundation is finished

    Contract source verified, 10% of supply burned in two phases, ownership renounced for good, bilingual lessons published, and Whitepaper V5 set as the final direction.

  4. Now

    Building the community

    A structured Telegram community comes first, before any invitation or promotion.

10%

Burned on-chain in two phases

Two burn phases removed 10% of the original supply through the contract. Ownership was then renounced, which permanently closed both minting and the contract burn function. Nobody can ever create new TMN.

Any future supply removal will be a transfer from founder wallets to a public burn address, with every transaction hash published on this page. These transfers take tokens out of circulation but don't change the totalSupply figure the contract shows, and we will describe them that way.
