DiToman (TMN) • BNB Smart Chain

TMN is the participation layer of Ditoman.

TMN is a BEP-20 token on BNB Smart Chain. It is not the point of Ditoman, it is a participation tool inside an education-first project. The contract source is verified on BscScan, ownership is renounced, and 10% of supply has been burned on-chain. Everything on this page can be checked independently.

Supply 10%

Burned on-chain in two phases

Two burn phases removed 10% of the original supply through the contract. Ownership was then renounced, which permanently closed both minting and the contract burn function. No new TMN can ever be created by anyone.

Any future supply removal will be done by transferring tokens from founder-held wallets to a public burn address, with every transaction hash published here. These transfers remove tokens from circulation but do not change the totalSupply figure shown by the contract, and we will describe them that way.

Honest Disclosure

What you should know

  • TMN has no meaningful liquidity or trading activity at present.
  • Supply is highly concentrated: the large majority sits in founder-controlled wallets, visible to anyone on BscScan.
  • The described utility is planned, not operational yet.
  • The project is run by its two founders, at small scale.
  • TMN is not listed on CoinGecko or CoinMarketCap.

This concentration reflects the token's history, deployed quietly in 2022 and never sold or promoted, not a distribution strategy. We state it plainly because our own lessons teach people to look for exactly this.
