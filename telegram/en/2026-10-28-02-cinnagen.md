🧬 Built Under Pressure | An Iranian medicine, with European approval

The European Commission has granted official marketing authorisation to Zandoriah, an osteoporosis medicine made by the Iranian company CinnaGen. This is CinnaGen's first European authorisation.

💊 What is Zandoriah?
Its active ingredient is teriparatide, and it is an equivalent version (biosimilar) of Eli Lilly's Forsteo. Most osteoporosis medicines only slow down bone loss, but this one stimulates the building of new bone.

🩺 How much does it help patients?
In the medicine's main trial (New England Journal of Medicine, 2001) of 1637 postmenopausal women:
• New spinal fractures fell by 65%
• Other fractures fell by 53%

🇪🇺 What does European approval mean?
• One authorisation for 30 countries: the 27 EU members + Iceland, Norway and Liechtenstein
• Approval by the European Medicines Agency's scientific committee: 26 February 2026
• Official authorisation: 27 April 2026
• The authorisation holder is CinnaGen's Portuguese subsidiary in Madeira
• Sales still have to start country by country, with pricing and insurance coverage

📉 Europe's need
About 4.3 million fractures caused by osteoporosis a year, more than €56 billion in costs, and 71% of women who should be treated are not being treated.

🏭 CinnaGen
• Founded in 1994; first biosimilar in 2006, in cooperation with Germany's Fraunhofer
• European GMP certificate since 2018
• According to the company: more than 19 biosimilars on the market and operations in more than 40 countries, from Algeria and Morocco to Vietnam and Ecuador

👩‍🔬 The board is chaired by Dr Haleh Hamedifar, who has worked at CinnaGen since 2002 and has headed the group since 2023.
