🏠 A lamp with a robot: Syncere says its lamp folds laundry and makes the bed

Syncere, a startup in Palo Alto, California, has built a lamp called Lume that gives light during the day and is also a home robot: it folds laundry, makes the bed, tidies the room and picks things up. TIME magazine has named it one of the "Best Inventions of 2026", and the company has just raised $11 million in seed funding.

⚙️ What do we know?
🔹 Price from $2,699; the two-unit model (Duet) is $5,199
🔹 Tasks: folding laundry, loading and unloading the dishwasher, tidying the room, making the bed, fetching items
🔹 Its light has two modes: focused light for reading and ambient light
🔹 To teach it your preferences, you wear a "personalisation band" on your wrist and show it the task
🔹 If someone or something is in its way, it stops
🔹 Investors: $11 million led by a16z Speedrun, with Draper Associates and several other funds
🔹 The company says it has millions of dollars in pre-orders

⚠️ But before the excitement
▫️ A second cost: for updates and new tasks you pay $79 a month or a one-off $679
▫️ Delivery timing is unclear: one source says deliveries start in the first quarter of 2027, with priority for New York and the San Francisco Bay Area; another source wrote "before the end of 2026". One source also gives a price of $1,999, which does not match the others
▫️ The demo videos come from the company itself and no independent test has been published; folding speed, the number of items per load and details of the battery and camera have not been disclosed
▫️ A camera and a robot in the home raise serious privacy questions; the company has not yet published full details

🔍 Ditoman's view
"Humanoid" home robots are still expensive and complex, so this company has taken another route: it brings the robot into the home in the form of a familiar object, a lamp, and adds capabilities through software and a subscription. If its promises hold up in real homes, that is interesting; if not, it is just a $2,700 lamp.
