🦠 Ebola: the largest outbreak in history. How big is the risk for us?

The Ebola outbreak in Congo has passed 10,000 confirmed cases, and nearly 4,900 people have died. Kenya and Nigeria have also had a few cases.

✅ What we should know:
• No cases have been reported in Iran, Turkey or the Middle East.
• The European Centre for Disease Prevention and Control considers the risk to the general public "very low".
• Ebola is not airborne; it spreads only through direct contact with a sick person's blood and body fluids.
• There is still no proven vaccine for this species of the virus, and trials are continuing.

📌 If you have returned from Congo, Uganda or Kenya and develop a fever or severe weakness within 21 days, tell a doctor immediately.
