🌍 Which other countries build equipment like this?
Industrial reactors and heavy petrochemical equipment are designed and built in a handful of countries:
▫️ Germany: thyssenkrupp Uhde, in the design of polymer units and high-pressure equipment
▫️ Japan: UBE Machinery, a maker of polymerisation reactors and process vessels, and Japan Steel Works, with a track record in building petrochemical reactors
▫️ China: which the Iranian manufacturer also mentioned
This comparison does not mean the models are the same. Reactors differ a great deal in size, pressure, material and process; a fair technical and price comparison needs the full specifications of both sides.

🔧 How much does it make Iran self-sufficient?
Building this equipment means the ability to make an important part of heavy equipment at home; but on its own it does not mean full self-sufficiency for a petrochemical unit. Besides the reactor, a polymer unit needs process design and licensing, catalysts, compressors, specialised valves, instrumentation, a control system and laboratory services.
The next important question: what share of the design, materials, parts and testing for this project was done in Iran, and what part came from abroad? Without that, the level of localisation cannot be stated.
