🏗 The Iran that was built: from homes to factories

Every home, shop, school and industrial shed we see today is the work of engineers, architects, workers, builders and families who stood behind it. This time we look at it in numbers: how much has Iran built in 50 years?

🏠 Homes: nearly 6 times in 50 years
Occupied housing units in the Statistical Centre of Iran's censuses (millions):
▫️ 1345 (1966): 3.9
▫️ 1355 (1976): 5.3
▫️ 1365 (1986): 8.3
▫️ 1375 (1996): 10.8
▫️ 1385 (2006): 15.9
▫️ 1395 (2016): 22.8
In the 20 years from 1375 to 1395 alone, this number grew 2.1 times. In 1395, about 13.1 million homes were in steel-frame or concrete buildings; five years earlier the figure was 8.8 million.
📌 In Mordad 1405 (July–August 2026), the deputy minister of roads and urban development put the country's total housing units at 32.5 million (including about 2.5 million empty units, most of them second homes). This figure is defined differently from the census and should not be compared with it directly.

🏭 Factories and industrial estates
▫️ 52,000 industrial units have started operating in industrial estates and zones (Iran Small Industries and Industrial Parks Organisation, 1404); about 45,500 are active
▫️ 92% of these units are small or micro; 3,064 are medium-sized and 899 are large
▫️ Every inactive unit is still a shed, facilities and prepared land that could be switched back on

🧱 The materials that built Iran
▫️ Cement: 68 million tonnes in 2025; sixth in the world, with 85 million tonnes of clinker capacity (USGS)
▫️ Crude steel: 31.8 million tonnes in 2025; tenth in the world (worldsteel)

📐 The building continues
In 1399 (2020/21), municipalities issued 174,000 building permits covering 93.6 million square metres of floor area.

🏫 Schools, hospitals, shops, warehouses
According to officials, more than 100,000 schools, more than a thousand hospitals, millions of trade units and hundreds of thousands of warehouses are active in the country. We have not yet confirmed these from the primary official source and are following up on the exact figures separately.

🌍 Why are we not giving a ranking yet?
Countries do not count buildings the same way. Pakistan's 2023 census counted 38.3 million "structures"; Iran counts "housing units". A 100-unit tower is 100 units in our statistics, but one building in a count of structures. So for now we show the scale, not a ranking.

🔍 Ditoman's view
Iran has a huge stock of built capital, the result of several generations of work. The next chapter is not building more; it is maintaining, renovating, strengthening and making better use of this same capital: safer homes, factories that come back to life and cities that work better. We will follow this path together.
