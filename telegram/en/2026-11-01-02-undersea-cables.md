🌊 Russian fishing vessels over Britain's undersea cables: a threat to Britain?

The British newspaper The Telegraph wrote in an investigative report (29 Mehr [21 October 2026]) that Russian fishing vessels have repeatedly been seen above undersea communication cables around Britain.

🔎 Reporters compared four and a half years of satellite tracking data for the vessels with maps of the cables:
🔹 17 vessels, more than 500 times at very low speed along cable routes
🔹 337 hours above the Atlantic Crossing 1 cable in northern Scotland
🔹 Manoeuvres near Google's Grace Hopper cable and several other cables

🗣 NATO has said these vessels are being monitored; Russia has previously denied any threat to the cables. Tracking data alone does not prove espionage.

💡 Why does it matter? Most of the world's internet traffic and financial transactions pass across the seabed. In Britain, about £1.4 trillion of transactions a day depend on these cables.
