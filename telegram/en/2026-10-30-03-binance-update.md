🔄 Binance hack update: the figure is out

Last night we wrote that the size of the theft was still unknown. Now Binance has published the first findings of its own investigation:

• Total losses are estimated at about $6.3 billion; this is the biggest hack in crypto history.
• About $3 billion of it has been frozen on-chain (frozen, not yet recovered).
• According to Binance, the laptop of one of its engineers was most likely hacked, and the attacker used it to send unauthorised withdrawal instructions; they have found no sign that wallet private keys were leaked.
• Binance says it will cover the rest of the losses from its users' emergency fund (SAFU) and its own resources.
• Withdrawals are expected to reopen within the next 48 hours.

💡 Today's lesson: an exchange's security is not just about its wallets; it is about every person and device that has access to it. The biggest hack in crypto history, according to Binance itself, started with one laptop.
