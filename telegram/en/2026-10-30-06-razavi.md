🔥 From Tehran to Anthropic's board

Anthropic, the maker of the AI Claude, has published its filing for an initial public offering on the US stock market, and it is now valued at an estimated $1.5 trillion or more. Among the members of its board, one name is familiar to us: Yasmin Razavi.

👤 Who is she?
She grew up in Tehran, studied engineering at the University of Toronto and earned an MBA from Harvard. She worked at McKinsey and at the Index Ventures fund in London, and was a product manager at Snapchat. Today she is a partner at the investment fund Spark Capital, and previously led investments in Cruise and Discord.

💡 Why does it matter?
In 2023, when Anthropic was valued at about $5 billion, Razavi became its first institutional investor and led a $450 million round. Since then Spark has put a total of $777 million into Anthropic, and because of her board seat, her name appears more than 50 times in the IPO filing.
