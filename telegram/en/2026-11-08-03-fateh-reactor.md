🇮🇷 A 500-tonne giant made in Iran: from a Shiraz workshop to the heart of the petrochemical industry (1 of 2)

Imagine a steel structure roughly as long as a 13-storey building is tall, weighing about 500 tonnes, built to work at the heart of a petrochemical plant.

According to reports published in Mehr 1405 (September–October 2026), such a reactor has been built by Iranian specialists at the Fateh Sanat company's workshop in Shiraz and is ready to be transported to Asaluyeh and installed. Some reports name the client as Arya Sasol Petrochemical; the official report does not name the client.

📐 Announced specifications
▫️ Weight: about 500 tonnes
▫️ Length: about 40 metres; diameter: 9 metres
▫️ Build time: about 14 months (according to one industry report)
▫️ Rolling of steel plate up to about 310 millimetres thick
Mehdi Ghotbi, head of the manufacturer, says the reactor was built entirely by Iranian specialists and that, in his words, until now only Japan, Germany and China had the technology to build a reactor like this. This is the manufacturer's claim, and we did not find an independent list to confirm it.

🧪 This structure is not just a big tank
It is called a polymerisation reactor: equipment in which raw materials are turned into polymers through a controlled process.

🏭 What does a reactor have to do with our lives?
Polymers are a large family of materials that are part of everyday life for all of us: from water pipes and food packaging to car parts, wire and cable insulation, and some medical equipment.
Inside the reactor, temperature and pressure must be precisely controlled so the chemical reaction runs safely and the product reaches the required quality. The bigger the reactor, the thicker the plate, the more precise the welding and the stricter the inspection it needs.
The exact type of polymer, the operating pressure and the production capacity of this reactor have not yet been announced, so we cannot say specifically which products' raw materials it will make.

💵 How does this project create foreign-currency value for Iran? Three separate paths:

1️⃣ Less need for imports
If an Iranian complex buys an acceptable unit from a domestic manufacturer instead of a foreign reactor, part of the foreign currency that would have been spent abroad stays in the country. According to the manufacturer, the foreign price quoted for similar equipment was about four times the cost of building it domestically. We do not have access to the contracts; the real saving only becomes clear after subtracting the cost of imported materials, parts, services and equipment.

2️⃣ Foreign-currency income from exporting equipment and services
If an Iranian manufacturer can sell similar equipment, engineering services, parts or maintenance to foreign customers, foreign currency comes into the country. According to the "Energy Desk" (Miz-e Energy) report, the manufacturer's group has previously carried out projects in Oman, the Republic of Azerbaijan and Turkey. That does not mean this reactor is being exported, but it does show a track record in the regional market.

3️⃣ Exporting petrochemical products
If the plant produces and exports product after installation, those sales bring in foreign currency. For comparison, according to a 1402 (2023/24) report, Arya Sasol exported more than 70% of its output to Europe, Asia and other markets. This particular reactor's share cannot be calculated yet.
