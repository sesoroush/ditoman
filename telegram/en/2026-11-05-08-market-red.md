🔴 From chips to bitcoin: why did markets suddenly turn red?

Ditoman report | Thursday 14 Aban 1405, 5 November 2026

Today's market question: if the build-out of AI data centres slows, what happens to the profits investors have already counted on? Heavy selling in chip and AI infrastructure stocks dragged the US stock market down and spilled over into crypto. The exact contribution of each factor cannot be separated out from a single day's prices.

📉 Size of the drop (US market close)
🔹 Philadelphia Semiconductor Index: −12.25%

🔹 Nasdaq: −5.52% (about 11.9% below its record; entered "correction" territory; worst day since April 2025)

🔹 S&P 500: −4.36%

🔹 Dow Jones: −3.44% (about 1,800 points)

🔹 Nvidia about −9.3% and AMD about −17.3%

🔹 Bitcoin about −5.2% (below $76,000) and Ether about −7.5% (near $2,231); these are 24-hour changes, so the comparison window is not the same

⚡ What happened?
1️⃣ Profitability: Qualcomm reported a 26% drop in quarterly revenue from mobile chips and higher production and component costs, but kept its $5 billion data-centre revenue target for fiscal 2027. The mobile decline alone does not prove that AI demand is falling; the market has become more sensitive to the gap between "promised growth" and "achievable profit".

2️⃣ Data centres: after the US elections, wins by some opponents of expanding these centres (especially in Texas and Georgia) added to worries about local restrictions and project delays. No nationwide halt to construction has been confirmed. Reports also point to profit-taking on the year's gains and de-risking by professional and retail investors.

3️⃣ Leverage in crypto: according to CoinGlass, more than $1 billion in trading positions were liquidated in 24 hours, most of them bets on prices rising. This is the value of the positions closed, not a net outflow of money.

🔗 Why is it a chain reaction?
Construction delays can push back purchases of chips, servers and power equipment; when share prices are built on future profits, those profits arriving later brings today's price down. High interest rates also raise the cost of financing projects.

🧭 Not everything moved the same way
Gold rose about 0.7% and Brent crude about 3.9% (oil on worries about a possible deal or a continuation of the war in the Middle East). The yield on 10-year US Treasuries fell to about 4.84%, meaning bond prices rose; this is consistent with a shift in demand and lower risk appetite, although it does not prove the exact path of the money.

🇮🇷 For Iranian readers
The toman price of bitcoin does not depend only on its dollar price; domestic gold is also affected by the global ounce price, the exchange rate and the premium (bubble). Wall Street turning red does not directly decide where the dollar or gold go in Iran tomorrow.

🔭 Possible paths (analytical scenarios, without statistical probabilities)
🔹 Volatility and an attempt to stabilise: if forced selling eases and no new news of investment cuts arrives

🔹 A more lasting recovery: if orders and construction plans hold and profits are delivered

🔹 A deeper correction: if project delays or cancellations are confirmed and profit estimates and financing come under pressure

A one-day jump does not prove a lasting recovery; nor does a one-day crash prove the start of a recession.

⏰ The next test: the US jobs report, Friday 6 November, 13:30 Lisbon (17:00 Tehran). Very strong wages and employment would raise interest-rate worries, and severe weakness would raise growth worries; the market's reaction depends on the mix of data.
