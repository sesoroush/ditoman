🏦 Crypto and AI: two engines rebuilding the financial system

Brian Armstrong, CEO of Coinbase, says the financial services industry is facing two technological transformations at the same time: crypto and artificial intelligence. In his view, this transformation is happening fast.

He predicts that "agentic finance" will process between $3 and $5 trillion in transactions by 2030. Agentic finance means smart software that buys, pays and transfers money on the user's behalf.
