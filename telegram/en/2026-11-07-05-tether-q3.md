💵 Who keeps the interest earned behind your 100 Tether?

If you hold 100 Tether (USDT) in your wallet for a year, your balance stays at 100 Tether; you earn nothing. Over the same period, the Tether company earns income from the assets that back these tokens.

On 30 October Tether announced about $1.5 billion in net operating profit for the third quarter of 2026, and $4.5 billion for the first 9 months of the year. According to the company, most of this income came from US Treasury bills and "reverse repo".

⚙️ How does the money turn into income?
When Tether issues new tokens directly, the dollars it receives go into its reserves. (If you buy Tether from someone else on an exchange, your money goes to the seller and only the token's owner changes.) Tether does not leave these reserves idle:
🔹 It buys short-term US Treasury bills and collects the interest
🔹 In "reverse repo" it lends money for a short period, takes government securities as collateral and earns the difference in the repurchase price
The interest belongs to the company, not the token holder. It is like a bank that takes deposits and pays no interest on them.

📊 How big is this business? (as of 30 September)
🔹 US Treasuries, direct and indirect: $132.4 billion; for comparison, total Treasuries recorded for South Korea in the August data were $134.2 billion
🔹 Total reserves: $191.45 billion; liabilities (mainly USDT in circulation): $183.50 billion
🔹 Excess reserves: $7.95 billion (end of June: $4.1 billion)
🔹 The rest of the reserves: gold and precious metals about $19 billion, bitcoin $8.4 billion, secured loans $14.2 billion

🧮 A back-of-the-envelope calculation (hypothetical): if $132.4 billion earned a 4% annual yield for the whole quarter, the gross quarterly return would be about $1.32 billion. When short-term rates fall, the new bills that replace maturing ones yield less; Tether's income is tied to US monetary policy.

⚠️ Profit, financial result and surplus: three separate numbers
▫️ "Operating profit" is the figure from Tether's own announcement
▫️ The overall "financial result" also includes gains and losses on gold and bitcoin; according to the report accompanying BDO's opinion, it was $1.66 billion for the 9 months. In the second quarter, despite $1.5 billion of operating profit, the overall result was a loss of about $4.2 billion (Ledger Insights)
▫️ BDO's opinion covers the figures and reserves at a specific date; it is not a full audit of performance

🇮🇷 What does this mean for an Iranian user?
👛 Wallet: Tether is centralised and can block USDT transfers from specific addresses, even in a personal wallet. This very week (2 November) it announced that it had frozen more than 1.5 billion USDT after the Binance hack. Holding a wallet's keys means controlling the wallet, not controlling the token itself
🏛 Sanctions: Tether does not provide its direct services to residents of Iran and blocks addresses on the US sanctions list. Tether is a "dollar outside the bank", but its backing sits right inside the US financial system
🏦 Bank: a bank deposit usually comes with insurance and interest; Tether has neither. The issuer risk is yours, the income is the company's
💱 The toman value of the same 100 Tether rises and falls with the dollar rate; Tether does not protect you from swings in the dollar against the toman, it is the dollar

🔍 Ditoman's view
Behind a token with an almost fixed price is one of the most profitable interest-based businesses in the world. Tether is a useful tool for moving money and holding it short-term, but it is not a savings account: it is a claim on the assets of a private company, not dollars in a bank.
