📉 Intel's worst day in 52 years

Intel: the company that built the world's first commercial microprocessor in 1971, and whose name was synonymous with "computer" for decades. On Friday its shares plunged 28.6%, its worst day since 1974.

🔹 What happened?
• A revenue forecast of $15 to $16 billion for next quarter, about 9% below market expectations ($17.1 billion)
• Profit margin fell from 44% to 38%, and the earnings-per-share forecast was $0.25, against expectations of $0.43
• Sales of PC chips fell 5%

🔹 The strange part
Intel's data-centre and AI revenue grew 78%. In other words, the company is growing in the right area, but the cost of building that future is eating into its profit for now.

🔹 It wasn't just Intel
The same day, the chip index fell 7.2%, the Nasdaq 2.86% and the S&P 500 1.82%.
