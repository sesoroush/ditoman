🔒 TMN liquidity is locked; what does that mean?

Today we locked, for one year, all the liquidity tokens of the TMN/BNB pool that were in the project's main wallet. We turned this into a lesson.

💧 What is liquidity?
For a token to be bought and sold, someone has to put TMN and BNB side by side in a pool. This pool is the token's liquidity.

🧾 An LP token is a receipt
Anyone who adds money to the pool receives an "LP token": a receipt they can use to take back their share whenever they want.

⚠️ The risk: a rug pull
If the receipt is held by the project team, they can empty the pool overnight and leave holders with a token they cannot sell.

🔒 What does a lock do?
It places the receipt, until a set date, in a contract that nobody, not even us, can open early.

✅ What we did

❗️ What a lock does not prove
Not the token's value, nor the project's future. TMN's supply is still concentrated in the founders' wallets; check this for yourself on BscScan too.
