💡 Built Under Pressure | The heart of the PET bottle chain is now Iranian

At the Morvarid petrochemical plant in Asaluyeh, an Iranian-made ethylene oxide (EO) catalyst was loaded into the glycol unit, and production reached 100% of capacity. According to the project's managers, only 6 countries in the world make this catalyst.

🔬 This catalyst converts ethylene with oxygen into ethylene oxide, from which glycol (MEG) is produced: the main material in PET bottles and polyester fibres. About a third of the weight of every bottle of mineral water, from Tehran to Lisbon, is this glycol.

⚙️ Making it carries high technical risk, which is why most plants import it.

💰 Morvarid's capacity is about 500,000 tonnes of glycol a year; at the Asian contract price ($880 a tonne), that means a product worth about $440 million a year, now produced on a domestically made catalyst.
