₿ 9 pages that changed the world 18 years ago

Today, 31 October, marks exactly 18 years since Satoshi Nakamoto published the Bitcoin whitepaper: a paper of just 9 pages with a simple idea, electronic money that moves directly between two people, without a bank and without an intermediary.

🔹 From that day to today
• 2008: the whitepaper is published, in the middle of the global financial crisis
• 2009: the first block is created, with a newspaper headline about bank bailouts recorded in it
• 2010: the first real purchase; 10,000 bitcoins for two pizzas
• 2024: bitcoin ETFs are approved in the US

🔹 Why does it still matter?
Bitcoin's cap is fixed: 21 million units, not one more. No bank, government or company can change this rule; and this "transparent, verifiable rule" was the core idea of that paper.
