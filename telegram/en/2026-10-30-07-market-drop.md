📉 Why did gold and US stocks fall together today?

The S&P 500 fell about 1.5% and the Nasdaq about 2.4%. Gold also dropped about 2.6% to around $3917, its lowest level since Tir (June–July). Three main reasons:

1. Disappointing results from tech giants: Alphabet shares had their worst day in more than 20 years, Intel fell about 28% and Amazon's free cash flow turned negative. The market is worried that heavy spending on AI has not yet turned into profit.

2. Inflation and the chance of a rate hike: the PCE inflation index posted its biggest monthly rise since March, and US economic growth was also strong. The market now sees a rate increase in December as likely. Higher interest rates are bad news for gold, which pays no yield.

3. An oil jump: after an attack on a tanker in the Strait of Hormuz, Brent rose about 3%. More expensive oil means more inflation and more pressure on the Federal Reserve.
