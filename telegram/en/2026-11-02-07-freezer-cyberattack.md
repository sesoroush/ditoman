🧊 They close up at night; by morning the freezers are at 50 degrees

This scene is repeating itself in supermarkets across Europe. A month after France, a new wave of suspected cyberattacks has hit refrigeration systems, and food has been thrown away.

🔹 October, France: about 700 stores
🔹 2 November: more than 100 stores in Belgium, Ireland, Sweden, Luxembourg and Estonia

❓ Who is behind it?
It is still unknown, and no group has claimed responsibility. A European commissioner called it "a deliberate attempt to disrupt the food supply chain". A security researcher also says that from a defender's point of view, "a teenager and an intelligence agency look almost the same".
