🏛 Iran's 30 sites on UNESCO's World Heritage List

Iran now has 30 World Heritage Sites: 28 cultural and 2 natural. The story began in 1979 with Persepolis, Naqsh-e Jahan Square and Tchogha Zanbil, then 24 years of silence, and since 2003 a new site almost every year. The newest is Alamut Castle and six related fortresses in Qazvin, inscribed in summer 2026.

✨ A few surprises few people know
▫️ Shahr-e Sukhteh: the oldest known artificial eye, about 4,800 years old
▫️ Maymand: a village whose people still spend winter in homes carved into the mountain
▫️ Lut Desert: where satellites have recorded the hottest land-surface temperatures on Earth
▫️ Trans-Iranian Railway: one of very few railways on the list
▫️ Bisotun: the key to reading cuneiform
