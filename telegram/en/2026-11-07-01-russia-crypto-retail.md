🇷🇺 Russia gives Bitcoin trading the green light, but with a bank lock

Russia's central bank has published the final rules: from 17 November 2026, ordinary people can legally buy Bitcoin, Ether and Tether (USDT), but only through licensed intermediaries such as banks and brokers.

📌 What happened?
🔹 Only 3 assets for non-professional investors: BTC, ETH and USDT
🔹 Purchase limit: 300,000 roubles a year per intermediary (about $3,650)
🔹 Everyone must pass a test before trading; "qualified" investors have no limit
🔹 Legal basis: the digital currency law that took effect on 1 September

🗓 Since when?
The law: passed in July, in force on 1 September. 11 August: approval of the 3 assets. 6 November: publication of the rules. 17 November: implementation. 1 December: trial start on the Moscow Exchange and Sberbank's plan to launch its products. 17 November is the day the rule takes effect, not the day every app opens.

🚫 Still banned
▫️ Paying for goods inside Russia with crypto
▫️ Transfers to personal wallets: assets must stay with the intermediary
▫️ P2P trading without an authorised intermediary, from 1 July 2027
(Settling foreign trade in crypto is allowed)

💰 Who benefits most?
Large banks and the exchange (fees and custody), the state (tax and regulatory visibility), foreign-trade companies; and ordinary people with a small limit, about 25,000 roubles a month. The losers: informal exchangers and P2P trading.

🔍 Ditoman's view
Russia has moved from "banned or grey" to an "institutionalisation" stage, but on its own model: control first, then access. Crypto becomes a supervised financial asset, not a payment tool or a means of financial independence. This is our reading, not the text of the rules.

⚠️ It is not yet clear whether the limit is combined across several intermediaries. The details come from media reports based on the central bank's statement. Not investment advice.
