1.Origin

Ditoman did not begin as a marketing concept. It began with a currency: the Toman, the everyday name Iranians use for their money, and the origin of “Digital Toman”, or DiToman.

In February 2022, TMN was deployed on BNB Smart Chain with a simple question behind it: could a token demonstrate that knowledge itself carries real value for Iranians, a population that has spent nearly five decades navigating sanctions, currency collapse, and restricted access to the global financial system?

That question was not theoretical. The founding family has operated a manufacturing business inside Iran for more than forty years, employing a substantial workforce, continuing to build and operate through decades of financial pressure and regulatory hardship that were neither chosen nor endorsed. Ditoman is built on lived experience, not observation from a distance.

The idea originated with the majority founder and was built together with a co-founding partner. For nearly four years the project remained deliberately quiet: no marketing, no promotion, no attempt to attract holders. That period was research: understanding what would actually be useful, and waiting until the technical foundation could stand up to scrutiny before asking anyone to pay attention.

This document marks the end of that phase.

2.What Ditoman Is

Ditoman is a bilingual (Persian and English) education and media platform with three purposes:

  1. Practical financial and crypto literacy. Real, structured education on digital assets, blockchain, and financial systems, written for people who need genuine understanding rather than trading signals or hype. Published in both languages, serving readers directly while supporting bilingual fluency.
  2. Honest analysis of technological and economic change. How artificial intelligence, automation, and energy transitions will reshape work and opportunity, examined specifically through the position of people who face these shifts with fewer institutional safety nets, rather than as abstract futurism written for no one in particular.
  3. Documentation of real achievement. Specific, verifiable accounts of what Iranian people and businesses have actually built and accomplished through years of economic difficulty, inside the country and across the diaspora.

3.Why This Is Needed

International coverage of Iran is dominated by geopolitics, sanctions, and conflict. This is a simple observation about what gets reported and what does not, not a claim about any political dispute. The everyday reality of businesses operating, factories running, engineers and scientists working, and families building lives rarely reaches the world, and rarely reaches Iranians themselves in a form that lets them recognise each other’s capability clearly.

At the same time, Persian-language crypto content online is dominated by trading signals and speculation. Structured, honest financial education without a product to sell is scarce, while the need for it is unusually high: currency devaluation, inflation, and restricted banking access mean that understanding digital money is not a hobby for this audience but a practical necessity.

Ditoman exists to close both gaps.

4.Editorial Position

Ditoman does not take positions on government policy, sanctions policy, or international disputes, from any side. This is a permanent editorial rule, not a temporary stance.

Where policy or economic conditions appear in Ditoman’s work, they appear only as context for what people have navigated, never as a subject of praise or blame. The value Ditoman offers is knowledge and accurate documentation, and neither requires agreement on contested politics to be useful.

What Ditoman does hold to: people’s achievements belong to those people, and are worth seeing clearly.

5.What Makes Ditoman Different

Ditoman is not a general futurism channel, not a trading-signals service, and not a political commentary page. It is built by people with direct standing in the community it serves, operating from outside Iran with the freedom to publish openly, working across both sides of the diaspora divide.

The token was deployed in 2022 and left deliberately inactive for years while the direction was worked out. That history is verifiable on-chain and is itself evidence of intent: this was never structured for a launch pump.

7.The Role of TMN

TMN is a utility and participation token. It is not the destination of the project and is not presented as a speculative promise.

Its intended role is to connect learning and contribution with recognition and access inside the Ditoman ecosystem. Its value is meant to track the strength of the education and community it supports, not the other way around.

Two supply reductions

  • Step 1, 24 April to 14 May 2026. 2,100,000,000,000 TMN (10% of original supply) were sent to the public dead address 0x…dEaD. No one holds its key, so these tokens can never move again, but they still count inside totalSupply.
  • Step 2, 13 September 2026. 2,098,943,554,216.15 TMN (about 10%) were burned through the contract’s own burn function. This step reduced totalSupply from 21,000,000,000,000 to 18,901,056,445,783.85.

Ownership of the contract was renounced after the second step.

Contract properties

The contract is a standard BEP-20 implementation derived from widely used open-source patterns. It contains:

  • No transfer tax or fee mechanism
  • No blacklist or address-freezing capability
  • No pause function
  • No proxy or upgradeable logic
  • No hidden owner privileges

Both the _mint and _burn functions are restricted by an onlyOwner modifier. Because ownership has been permanently renounced, both functions are now unreachable by any party, including the founder.

The consequences are stated plainly:

  • No new TMN can ever be created. The maximum supply is permanently fixed at its current level. This guarantee cannot be reversed by anyone.
  • The contract-level burn function can no longer be called. Total supply as recorded on-chain will not decrease further through that mechanism.

This was a deliberate trade. Permanently eliminating the possibility of new issuance was judged more valuable than retaining the burn function, particularly for a token whose supply remains concentrated. It is irreversible, and it is disclosed here rather than left for readers to discover.

9.Supply, Distribution and Transparency

Current reality

TMN supply remains highly concentrated. In October 2026 there were 33 holders, and the large majority of supply sits in founder-controlled wallets. This is visible to anyone on BscScan and is stated here directly rather than minimised.

This concentration reflects the project’s history, a token deployed quietly in 2022 and never distributed or promoted, not a distribution strategy. It is the starting condition, not the intended end state.

On 29 October 2026 the TMN/BNB liquidity tokens held in the project’s main wallet were locked until 29 October 2027. That liquidity cannot be withdrawn before then.

Path forward

  • Supply removal from circulation. Because the contract burn function is now permanently inaccessible, any future supply reduction will be executed by transferring tokens from founder-held wallets to a verifiable burn address, permanently removing them from circulation. Such transfers do not reduce the totalSupply figure recorded by the contract; the tokens remain visible at the burn address and are unrecoverable by anyone. Ditoman commits to describing this accurately rather than as a contract burn.
  • Staged and documented. Future removals will be executed in stages tied to ecosystem milestones, with each transaction hash published so that anyone can verify it independently.
  • Distribution through participation. Meaningful decentralisation comes from tokens reaching real participants, not from supply reduction alone. Distribution is intended to follow genuine learning and contribution within the ecosystem rather than sale or airdrop to passive holders.
  • No timeline promises that cannot be kept. This document deliberately avoids fixed dates for supply actions. Commitments stated here are ones the project intends to meet and be judged on.

11.Roadmap

Phase 1: Foundation (complete). Contract source code verified on BscScan. Ownership renounced. About 20% of supply removed from circulation in two on-chain steps. Bilingual educational publishing established. This whitepaper finalised as the settled direction.

Phase 2: Community Structure (in progress). Telegram community launched with real structure (published rules, seeded educational content, clear onboarding) before any invitation or promotion. All content aligned to the three pillars.

Phase 3: Controlled Growth. Direct participation in existing Persian-language crypto and diaspora communities. Limited, measured distribution testing. First documented supply-removal milestone, published with transaction proof.

Phase 4: Learn-and-Earn Pilot. Small-scale reward mechanism tied to verifiable learning activity, fully transparent and documented. Public review of progress against this roadmap, including honest reporting of what did not work.

12.Risk and Honest Disclosure

Readers should understand the following clearly:

  • Supply is concentrated among very few holders.
  • The described utility is planned, not operational.
  • The project is currently operated by its two founders, at small scale.

Digital assets carry substantial risk. Nothing in this document is financial or investment advice, and nothing here should be read as a promise of future value.

Ditoman’s position is that stating these facts plainly is more valuable than obscuring them.

13.Closing

Ditoman is not built around noise, and it is not built around a political argument. It is built on a straightforward conviction: that people navigating genuine economic difficulty deserve real knowledge, honest analysis of what is coming, and an accurate record of what they have already achieved, on their own terms.

Four years of quiet work produced the foundation. This document sets the direction that will not change again.

We are not building just a token. We are building a direction, and this is it.
